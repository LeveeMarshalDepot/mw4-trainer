How it works

From download to first match in under two minutes — no complicated setup required.

1

Download the injector

Grab the latest mw4-trainer build from gitscore.cc and extract the archive to any folder on your Windows 10 or 11 machine.

2

Launch MW4 and inject

Start Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4, then run the loader as administrator. It attaches to the game process and opens the overlay.

3

Toggle features with hotkeys

Press your configured hotkeys to enable aimbot, ESP, infinite ammo and more. The external overlay stays visible while you play.
